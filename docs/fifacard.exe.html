Description

A personalised FIFA Ultimate Team card printed on Foamex is a unique and durable way to showcase your favourite football player. Foamex is a high-quality plastic material that is lightweight, waterproof, and rigid,.Thus making it an ideal medium for printing your card.

Your personalised card will feature your image on the front, along with their name, position, and overall rating. You can also customize the card’s color scheme using the drop down menu.

On the you’ll also find a range of stats and information about your player, including their pace, shooting, passing, dribbling, defending, and physical attributes. You can also customise this stas along with their nationality and Club to bring a truly unique touch to your card.

The personalized FIFA Ultimate Team card printed on Foamex is perfect for displaying in your room, office, or any other space where you want to show off your love for the beautiful game. A great choice of gift for the football fan, its durable construction means it will withstand wear and tear, making it a great addition to your collection for years to come.

How your personalised Fifa Ultimate Team Card works:

Once ordered please send the image for the card along with the stats, nantionality, club and name to for your card to Print@cardboardboxes.co.uk
Do not worry about having a transparent or plain background on the image you provide as we will remove this as part of the service.However if possible please send this in a PDF or PNG format with the subject being your order number.
We do suggest that you use the below template for sending the information however if you do require different stats (eg if the recipent is a goalkeeper) please feel free to provide them and we will produce the card to your requirements.
Please note:  You can also request players to be printed on the cards, great for fans of particular players or teams!

Name|Position|Rating|Club|Nationality|Rating|PAC|DRI|SHO|DEF|PAS|PHY|
